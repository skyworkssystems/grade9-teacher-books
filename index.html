Installing

From download to teaching in four steps

The app file comes from this page straight to your phone. It takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Tap a download button

    Choose English or Social Science above. Your phone saves an .apk file — this is the app itself.

  2. 2

    Allow the install

    Android asks permission to install from your browser or Files app. Tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source.

  3. 3

    Open the file and install

    Find it in Downloads, tap it, then tap Install. The book icon appears with your other apps.

  4. 4

    Open it and teach

    Everything is already inside the app. It never asks for network again — in the classroom, at home, anywhere.

Questions

Before you download

Do I need internet to use the book?

Only to download the app once. After it is installed, the entire book — every lesson, test, exam and answer — is on your phone. It never downloads anything again, so it works the same whether your school has network or not.

Is it free?

Yes. Download it, install it, and use it. There is no sign-in, no subscription and no advertising in the app.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone running Android 6.0 or newer, which covers almost every phone in use. iPhone is not supported at the moment — on an iPhone, use a PDF exported from a colleague's Android phone, or the printed version.

My phone warns me before installing the file. Is that normal?

Yes. Android shows that warning for any app installed outside the Play Store, whatever the app is. Tap Settings, then Allow from this source, and continue. Only download the file from this page.

Does it cover the whole syllabus?

Yes. The 200 daily lesson plans together cover the complete standards-based syllabus for the grade and subject. Each lesson shows the strand and objective it comes from, so you can check the coverage yourself.

Can I print it for my students?

Yes. Export the book as a PDF and print it — a copy for yourself, and handouts for students who have no phone.

How do I get updates?

Come back to this page and download the newest version. Installing it over the old one keeps the app in place — there is no need to uninstall first.
